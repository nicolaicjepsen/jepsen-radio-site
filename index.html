A little radio for your Mac and iPhone

Jepsen Radio is an internet radio drawn as a vintage walnut set, in hand-made pixel art. Turn the knob, watch the needle sweep, press a preset. Stations from all over the world, on an FM dial you arrange yourself.

What it does

  • Thousands of live stations from the community directory radio-browser.info.
  • Your own FM band from 88.0 to 108.0: put each station where you like.
  • Five preset buttons, a tuning knob, static between stations, and a lamp that warms up.
  • Several dials for different moods, shareable by AirDrop.
  • Keeps playing in the background, with lock-screen and headphone controls.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking.
