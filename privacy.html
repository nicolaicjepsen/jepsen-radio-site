Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026. Applies to Jepsen Radio for Mac and iPhone.

Jepsen Radio is an internet radio. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer does not collect, receive, store or sell any information about you.

What stays on your device

Your stations, dials, presets, last frequency, volume and settings are saved on your Mac or iPhone so the radio is as you left it. They never leave your device unless you choose to share a dial file. Deleting the app deletes them.

Who the app talks to

To work as a radio, the app connects to two kinds of services. Like any internet connection, these servers can see your device's IP address. The developer has no access to anything they receive.

  • Radio Browser (radio-browser.info), a free community directory of radio stations. When you search for stations, your search words, chosen country and genre are sent to it. It is run independently of Jepsen Radio.
  • The radio stations you listen to. Playing a station streams its audio from the station's own server, which may also send the name of the song that's playing. Each station is run by its owner under its own policies.

Sharing dials

If you share a dial (a .jepsendial file with station names, addresses and frequencies) by AirDrop, Mail, Files or similar, it goes only where you send it.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children. Live radio stations choose their own content.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
